Our story

A stall's worth of grandmothers' recipes.

Grandma's PIE started as a simple idea: bring the langos stands of Budapest and the papanasi carts of Romania to a quiet corner of Antwerp, and make everything the slow way — dough rested, fried to order, never sitting under a heat lamp.

Every langos is stretched by hand and fried fresh per order, then topped the traditional way — garlic sauce and sour cream, or simply a dusting of powdered sugar the way it's sold on the street. The papanasi, a Romanian fried-dough dessert piled with sour cream and jam, is the one regulars come back for.

It's a small kitchen with a short menu, and everything on it is made to be eaten the same day it's made.
