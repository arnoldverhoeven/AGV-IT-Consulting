Our Heritage

Three generations of
Antwerp craftsmanship

Since 1990, Abai Jewellery has been a fixture of De Keyserlei, in the heart of Antwerp's historic diamond quarter — a city that has set the world's standard for cutting and trading diamonds for over five centuries.

What began as a small family counter has grown into a trusted address for collectors, brides, and connoisseurs seeking diamonds of proven provenance and jewels made by hand, not by machine.

Every piece that leaves our boutique carries that same conviction: a stone is only as fine as the trust behind it.

What We Do

Our Speciality

Diamonds

A hand-selected range of loose and set diamonds, chosen for cut, clarity and character rather than volume.

Handmade Jewels

Rings, necklaces and bespoke commissions shaped by hand in-house, built around the stone rather than a catalogue.

Diamonds with Certificate

Every diamond of significance is sold with independent certification, so provenance and grading are never in question.
