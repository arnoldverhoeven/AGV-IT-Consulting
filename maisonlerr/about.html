The vision behind Maison Lerr

A boutique event agency in Antwerp specialising in luxury celebrations. We believe every event should reflect the unique story of our clients.

Founded by Lerato Bellekens

A boutique event agency built on hospitality and human connection.

With a background in tourism, aviation and event management, founder Lerato Bellekens combines creativity with meticulous attention to detail.

Maison Lerr creates unforgettable experiences for every client and works with luxury brands across different industries. Our clients receive events that are elegant, memorable and professionally managed, with a team that focuses on creativity and attention to every detail.

We work closely with every client to understand their vision, manage every detail from planning to execution and make sure every celebration reflects our commitment to quality and creativity. Ultimately, our goal is simple: every guest should feel welcomed, valued and inspired.

Our story

How we create effortless experiences.

At Maison Lerr, we see every celebration as an opportunity to tell a story. Our passion lies in transforming ideas into immersive experiences that bring people together and create lasting memories.

From the first conversation to the final farewell, we approach every event with creativity, precision and a deep understanding of what makes moments meaningful. Exceptional events are built on thoughtful details, seamless coordination and a personal touch that reflects each client’s unique vision.

Whether an intimate gathering, a luxury wedding or a large-scale corporate event, we are dedicated to delivering experiences that feel both elegant and effortless — where every venue, design element and guest interaction is carefully curated.
