Who I am

I'm Tobias.

A tattoo artist with more than 10 years of experience, and a creative background that started long before I entered the tattoo world.

I have been drawing my entire life, from art classes as a child to painting courses and a long graffiti history. It all shaped my sense of line, rhythm and contrast.

As an adult, I founded the graphic design studio afreux.be with two friends. We worked in music, the cultural sector, NGOs and personal artistic projects. My years in design taught me to think in composition, balance, typography and visual clarity. These elements now flow naturally into my tattoo work.

My background is not a collection of unrelated experiences. It is one continuous path of drawing, shaping, observing and refining.

My style

Timeless, bold & designed for the body

I work within classic Western and American Traditional tattooing and combine this with a modern, graphic approach. A traditional base, on which a layer of personal influence and style is added. More modern imagery, objects and influences are mixed into the subtle outcome. I consciously remain very loyal stylewise through time — away from trends and fashion.

  • Clean lines
  • Solid colour
  • Strong contrasts
  • Long-term readability
  • Designs that follow the shape of the body

I always design with the healing process in mind. Lines and shapes are chosen because they hold up well on the skin, both today and many years from now. The pigments and my colour palette are chosen through years of experience to stay saturated and bright.

I do not follow fast-fading trends. I focus on craftsmanship that lasts. Placement — for both now and future plans — is always carefully taken into account, as well as making my work blend with that of other artists.

How I work

Honest, focused & one-on-one

In my private studio, I work with one client at a time. This gives us time to talk through your idea and space to refine it.

You can expect a calm and professional atmosphere, honest advice and clear communication without sales talk.

I see a tattoo as a collaboration. It is your idea combined with my experience, and we work together in a direct and open way. If it doesn't feel right, both me and the client can agree not to go further — luckily that rarely happens.

Aftercare

Healing

Keep in mind: good aftercare is part of a good end result. Four phases, a few do's and don'ts.

First hours

Foil & first wash

Keep the foil for at least three hours or overnight. Wash your hands with soap before handling your new tattoo.

Remove the foil and wash the tattoo carefully with your hands to remove all surface blood, sweat and ink. To avoid irritation from the tape, remove the foil after a few minutes of showering.

Clean with just water — pH-neutral soap can be used, but no normal soap. Rinse, then let it dry in open air or pat dry with a clean towel.

Day 1 – 14

Thin layer of cream

We advise Cicaplast B5 or Easy Tattoo. Always apply a thin layer of ointment, 3 to 6 times a day for at least 14 days to maintain hydration.

It helps the tattoo settle nicely if you keep going for an extra 2 weeks.

You may refilm for two nights to avoid stains in your bed — also if you have a dirty job. Keep your tattoo open for the rest of the time.

Day 3 – 7 and on

Peeling & scabbing

The tattoo may start to peel and scab. Don't pick or peel the skin away — let it fall off naturally during washing or applying cream. Fresh skin will soon replace the dead skin.

The ink may fade during the process, but will return when healing is completed. Too dry skin will crust and push ink out. With a real crust, increase cream frequency — but always apply slightly: too much prevents the skin from breathing.

After a few months

Sun

After a few months you may sunbathe in moderation.

Use sun cream, factor 50, to prevent early fading. Exposure to the sun is the major cause of tattoo fading.

Do

  • Change your bed sheets if you have pets entering the bedroom.
  • Continue applying cream as long as the tattoo feels sensitive.
  • Keep in mind good aftercare is part of a good end result!
  • Get in touch if your tattoo needs a touch-up.
  • Get in touch if you're worried about the healing process or have an infection.

Don't

  • Apply Vaseline or petroleum-based products.
  • Apply alcohol.
  • Rub, scratch or pick at the tattoo.
  • Expose the tattoo to direct sunlight.
  • Swim — no sauna, no steam bath or bathtub for 3 to 4 weeks.

Happy healing!
