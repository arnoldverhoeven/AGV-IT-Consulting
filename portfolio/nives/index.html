Nives

A bright, green breakfast and lunch spot with its own sourdough bakery by Harmoniepark — pitched as a bilingual NL/EN redesign concept.

View the concept ↗
Overview

A concept built to pitch a full redesign.

Nives is a real breakfast, lunch and bakery spot on the edge of Antwerp's Harmoniepark, run out of a converted orangery. This concept site was built as a redesign pitch — a light, airy layout that matches the space itself, a full bilingual NL/EN menu with seasonal dishes, and a photo-led story section built around the bakery and its owners.

Key features

What the concept actually does.

01

Bilingual NL / EN

Every section, including the full menu, is written in Dutch and English with a simple language toggle.

02

Seasonal menu, chalkboard-first

Breakfast, lunch and bakery items laid out clearly, with a note that the in-store chalkboard always has the final word.

03

Photo-led storytelling

A dedicated story section covering the building's history and the people behind the bakery, built around real photography.

04

Walk-in first

No booking system — the site makes clear this is a no-reservations, just-walk-in kind of place.
