Concept · Tattoo studio

Souvenir Tattoo Parlour

Bold lines, solid colour, tattoos that still look right in forty years — a traditional tattoo and piercing studio in the heart of Antwerp.

View the concept ↗
Overview

A gallery-first concept, built around the work.

Souvenir Tattoo Parlour is a traditional tattoo and piercing studio on Gierstraat, with a resident crew of four artists and a rotating line-up of international guests. This concept site puts the work itself front and centre — a bold gallery of flash and finished pieces, a roster of resident and guest artists, and a deliberately old-school "call to book" approach that matches how the studio actually operates.

Key features

What the concept actually does.

01

Work-led gallery

A large, scrollable gallery of finished tattoos leads the page — the studio's strongest sales tool, front and centre.

02

Artist roster

Each resident artist gets their own style description and Instagram link, so visitors can pick a hand that matches what they want.

03

Guest artist spotlight

A dedicated section for the studio's rotating international guest artists, pointing to Instagram for upcoming dates.

04

Phone-first booking

No online booking widget — a clear "call to book" pattern throughout, matching how the studio actually takes appointments.
