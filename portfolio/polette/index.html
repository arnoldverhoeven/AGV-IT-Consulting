Concept · Coffee bar

Polette

A corner coffee bar in Antwerp's Markgravewijk, built around specialty coffee, house-made pastries and a loyal neighbourhood following.

View the concept ↗
Overview

A concept built around the owner's own story.

Polette is a real coffee bar on Fransenplaats, opened in 2026 by a former fashion-industry professional turned full-time barista. This concept site leads with that story, backs it up with a full drinks menu and the bar's carrot cake reputation, and closes the trust loop with real Google reviews pulled straight from the neighbourhood.

Key features

What the concept actually does.

01

Founder story up front

The "why" behind the bar — a career change and a vacant corner shop — told as the centrepiece of the homepage, not an afterthought.

02

Full drinks menu

Espresso-based, iced and hot drinks laid out with prices, so visitors know what to expect before they walk in.

03

Real Google reviews

Five-star reviews from actual customers, built into the page as social proof rather than a generic testimonials block.

04

Route & opening hours

A clear map link and weekly hours, so the last step before visiting takes one tap.
