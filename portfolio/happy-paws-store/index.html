Happy Paws Store

"Alles begint met kwispelen" — a neighbourhood pet shop in Antwerp, now selling online through Shopzo alongside its physical store.

Visit the store ↗ See the Shopzo platform →
Overview

The Shopzo platform, proven with a real shop.

Happy Paws Store is the proving ground for the Shopzo platform: a real, independent pet shop selling food, toys and supplies both in Antwerp and online. The storefront runs entirely on Shopzo's shared infrastructure, while the owner manages the catalogue, stock and orders through the Shopzo seller dashboard — with no separate systems to keep in sync between the physical shop and the webshop.

Key features

What the storefront actually does.

01

Full product catalogue

Browsable by category, with featured products surfaced on the homepage and kept in sync with in-store stock.

02

Local trust signals

Google review scores, delivery promises and a straightforward returns policy, written in the shop's own voice.

03

Cart & checkout

A lightweight cart and checkout flow backed by Shopzo's shared payment and order infrastructure.

04

Dutch-first content

Copy and UX written for the shop's actual customer base in Antwerp, not translated from a template.
