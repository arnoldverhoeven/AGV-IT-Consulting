Overview

Two sides of the business, one page.

The AGV IT Consulting site carries two sides of the business on one page: security and infrastructure consultancy for pharma, logistics and enterprise clients, and web development, webshops and booking systems for local businesses. It's built as a single-page design with a light blue and navy palette, a numbered service breakdown, a full career track record, and a quote request form that opens a pre-filled email — no back-and-forth needed to get a conversation started.

Key features

What the site actually does.

Services, selected work, track record and certifications all live on one scrollable page, tied together with anchor navigation.

The contact form builds a ready-to-send email on submit, so getting a quote doesn't require setting up an account.
