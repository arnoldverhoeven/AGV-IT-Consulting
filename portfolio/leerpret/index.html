Leerpret

Overview

A quiz platform built for the front of the classroom.

Leerpret turns a lesson into a shared moment: one click starts an interactive quiz, and the whole class joins in on the big screen. Topics span maths, language, geography, animals and more, aimed at grades one through six — built for teachers who want something ready to run, not another platform to configure.

Key features

What the site actually does.

01

One click, whole class

No logins or accounts for pupils — a quiz starts on the shared screen and everyone joins at once.

02

Grade-appropriate topics

Content organised by subject and grade level, from first to sixth grade, so teachers find the right quiz fast.

03

Built for the big screen

Layouts designed for a projector or smartboard at the front of the room, not a phone or tablet screen.

04

Broad subject range

Maths, language, geography and animals sit alongside each other, covering a real school week rather than one subject.
