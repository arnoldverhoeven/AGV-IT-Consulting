Overview

A concept built around a real, recurring need.

Selling or renting a property in Belgium means gathering a stack of mandatory certificates: an asbestos attest, an EPC energy rating, electrical and gas inspections, an oil-tank check, a property condition report. This concept site brings all of it under one certified inspector, with a self-service price estimate and a 24-hour quote turnaround — built to make a legally required, often confusing process feel manageable.
