PMEB

Project management, expertise and safety coordination for construction projects — a concept site for a firm based in Vlimmeren, Antwerpse Kempen.

View the concept ↗
Overview

A concept built for a BNI Kapellen showcase.

PMEB — Project Management en Expertises in de Bouw — advises on and manages construction projects, from safety coordination to expert reports on construction disputes. This concept site was originally built as a showcase piece for BNI Kapellen: a straightforward, trust-first layout aimed at builders, architects and property owners who need a clear point of contact for a technical, high-stakes decision.

Key features

What the concept actually does.

01

Service breakdown, in plain terms

Project management, expertise and safety coordination laid out as three distinct services, not one vague offering.

02

Built for the Kempen region

Location and service area front and centre, since construction work is inherently local and trust starts with proximity.

03

Credentials up front

Qualifications and experience surfaced early — the kind of reassurance a technical, high-value decision needs.

04

Direct, low-friction contact

A simple way to start a conversation, matching how construction professionals actually reach out — by phone or a short message.
